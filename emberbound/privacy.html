Emberbound is a browser-based fantasy role-playing game operated by StArcade9. This policy describes the current game and its Facebook Instant Games integration. For privacy questions or requests, contact brendonsmith@seacloud9.org.

Information used by the game

  • Saved progress: your selected hero, campaign and encounter progress, procedural world seed, equipment, inventory, shards and win totals.
  • Preferences: sound, reduced motion and screen-shake settings.
  • Support messages: if you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include.

Progress and preferences are stored in your browser on the device where you play. When you play through Meta and its player-storage APIs are available, the game may also store a copy of your progress in Meta's game storage to restore your progress later. Meta associates that storage with your platform account; Emberbound does not require a separate game account.

The current game does not request your Facebook name, profile picture, email address, friend list or precise location. It does not ask you to enter a Meta password or access token. The current game has no StArcade9 advertising, payment or third-party analytics integration.

How information is used

We use saved progress and preferences to run the game, remember your choices and continue your campaign. Meta storage, when available, helps restore your progress. We use information you send to support to answer your request and address technical or privacy issues.

Meta and hosting providers

Facebook and Messenger provide the surrounding platform, account services, SDK and hosting when you play an Instant Game. Meta may process account, device, network and usage information under its own policies. See Meta's Privacy Policy. Removing a game save does not delete your Facebook account or Meta's own records.

These public information pages are hosted with GitHub Pages. Website and platform providers may process technical request information, such as an IP address and browser information, when delivering their services. See GitHub's Privacy Statement. StArcade9 does not operate a separate server database of Emberbound gameplay progress.

Storage and retention

Your device keeps game data until you delete it, reset the relevant progress, or clear the game's browser storage. A Meta copy of progress may remain until it is cleared through the game or Meta's available controls. Support correspondence is retained as needed to handle your request and any applicable recordkeeping obligations. We do not sell Emberbound gameplay data.

Your choices and deletion

In the game, open Settings → Delete saved data and confirm. This removes saved progress and preferences from the current browser and clears the current Meta progress copy when that storage API is available. A new play session can create a new save. Repeat local deletion on other devices where you have played.

See the complete data-deletion instructions for alternatives, platform controls and support requests. You can also contact us to ask about data you supplied directly to StArcade9, including support correspondence. Where applicable law gives you access, correction, deletion, restriction or other privacy rights, contact us to make a request. We may ask for the minimum information needed to verify and process it.

Eligibility and changes

When using Facebook or Messenger, follow Meta's age and account eligibility requirements. Do not send us a child's personal information. If you believe personal information was provided improperly, contact us so we can review and address it.

If the game's data practices change, we will update this page and its effective date. Questions can be sent to brendonsmith@seacloud9.org.
