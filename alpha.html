[ 01 ] WHAT IS NOVA64?

A tiny creative machine for building retro 3D games.

Code with JavaScript, experiment with low-poly worlds, and publish playable experiences that feel like lost cartridges from another timeline.

Nova64 is a complete fantasy console — not just a framework. It gives you the editor, the runtime, the renderer, and a place for your games to live. Approachable enough for a first sketch, powerful enough for a full mini-game.
